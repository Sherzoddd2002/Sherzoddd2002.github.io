01 — About

Turning messy data into
decisions people can act on.

My work sits where data engineering, machine learning and product thinking overlap. I care less about notebooks that produce a nice chart and more about systems that stay honest under scrutiny — where every number carries its provenance, every model states its limits, and a failed hypothesis is documented as a finding rather than buried.

Over the last two years I've built agricultural yield-forecasting platforms on top of FAOSTAT, IFPRI MAPSPAM, NASA POWER / MODIS and Sentinel-2; a purged walk-forward quantitative research stack for BTCUSDT; and a gamified data-literacy academy with a real 3D world.

Every project here is public and reproducible — code, methodology, and the negative results included.

  • Reproducible — pipelines run end to end from raw source to published dashboard.
  • Honest — no validated claim without held-out evidence; limits stated up front.
  • Leakage-free — chronological splits, purged folds, untouched holdouts.
  • Accessible — static, dependency-light sites that run anywhere, even offline.

OSH Agriculture AI — Global

A location-first agricultural data and yield-forecasting platform. Users select a country and administrative area, enrich it with environmental datasets, run quality checks, and receive a clearly-labelled yield estimate.

  • 838,919 curated data rows across two independent sources
  • 245 country/territory boundary codes, ADM1 + ADM2
  • 153 countries · 46 crops in the MAPSPAM reference
  • 37 stationary model features, browser-side Ridge pipeline
  • 379 commits · 190k+ lines of Python

Scientific scope is explicit: MAPSPAM 2020 is a single reference-year gridded estimate, not a current-year observation or forecast. The model refuses to predict below 8 aligned observations and labels future seasons CLIMATOLOGY_SCENARIO. Validation uses expanding-window OOF benchmarks.

OSH Quant Lab — BTCUSDT H96 C2 Strategy

A fully transparent, purged walk-forward quantitative research system for BTCUSDT futures. The H96 C2 configuration achieved a net win rate of 58.92% with positive expectancy across development OOS testing — turning $100 into $121.06 (+21.06%) on paper trades, with a Profit Factor of 1.16 and 4 out of 5 folds profitable. Live orders remain disabled by design; all results are historical paper backtests.

  • 240,672 real 15m candles · 2020-01-01 → 2026-11-11 · 0 gaps
  • 241 OOS paper trades · 58.92% net win rate
  • +9.463 bps expectancy per trade · PF 1.1596
  • $100 → $121.06 (+21.06%) · max DD 15.84% · 4/5 folds positive (80%)
  • 6-fold controlled ablation · external data audit with correlation tests

Built for research integrity. The system uses triple-barrier labelling, purged walk-forward validation with 6-bar embargoes, Wilson 95% confidence intervals, and block-bootstrap significance testing. GO gates require positive median net, ≥75% positive folds, PF > 1, and bootstrap lower bound above zero. The forward paper runner is frozen on a separate branch pending holdout completion.

04 — Methodology

How I make sure the numbers are real.

These aren't aspirations — they're the specific rules the OSH research stack enforces automatically, and which every published figure in this portfolio passes through.

🧪

Leakage-free validation

Chronological expanding-window splits only. A 6-candle embargo separates train from test, and thresholds are calibrated on the train portion of each fold alone — never on the full validation set, never retrofitted.

🔒

Untouched holdouts

A 6-month holdout is opened once, at the very end, and never used for tuning or retraining. It exists to answer one question: did the edge survive contact with data the model had never seen?

📐

Statistical significance

Win rates carry Wilson 95% confidence intervals. Expectations are tested with block-bootstrap. A GO verdict requires positive median net, ≥75% positive folds, Profit Factor > 1, and a bootstrap lower bound above zero — all of them.

🔍

Data integrity audits

Every dataset is checked for duplicates, gaps, OHLC consistency and autocorrelation. When synthetic or proxy data is found, it is labelled as such — not quietly shipped.

💰

Cost realism

Backtests model 10 bps round-trip as the primary scenario, with a 7-point sensitivity sweep across commission regimes. If the strategy only works at zero costs, it doesn't work.

🧾

Provenance & licensing

FAOSTAT, MAPSPAM, geoBoundaries, SIAT, NASA and Open-Meteo are cited per dataset with units, version and licence. Third-party data retains its own licence; application code is MIT.
